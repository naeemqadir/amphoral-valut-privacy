Privacy Policy for Ephemeral

Application: Ephemeral - Smart Screenshot Hub & Action Vault
Package Name: com.ephemeral.screenshothub
Last Updated: October 2026

🛡️ Privacy-First Architecture: All screenshot text recognition (OCR) and smart data extraction are processed 100% locally on your device. Your screenshots and extracted data are never transmitted, saved, or uploaded to any remote server or third party.

This Privacy Policy describes how Ephemeral ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your media and personal data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Ephemeral is designed as a local-first utility. We minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary to provide our core features:

  • Screenshots & Media Files: The App accesses image files stored in your device's designated screenshot directory to display them in your feed, allow management, apply expiration timers, and purge expired screenshots according to your settings.
  • On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR): The App parses screenshot text locally using on-device machine learning to recognize dates, links, Wi-Fi credentials, and tracking numbers. This information never leaves your device.
  • Biometric Authentication: If you use the Action Vault, authentication is handled entirely through the official Android BiometricPrompt API. We do not collect, process, or store your fingerprint, face, or biometric data.
  • App Preferences & Timers: Your configuration options (such as custom auto-purge countdowns, favorite tags, and vault flags) are stored in an encrypted local database on your device.

2. Device Permissions and Why We Request Them

To provide its screenshot management functionality, Ephemeral requests the following permissions:

Permission Purpose
Photos & Media Storage
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Allows the App to detect new screenshots, display them inside the hub, and organize them into smart feeds.
Media Management / Deletion
MANAGE_MEDIA, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Enables seamless auto-purging of expired screenshots and single-tap deletion without cluttering your system with manual prompts.
Display Over Other Apps
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW		 Displays the optional floating action HUD right after a screenshot is taken so you can select retention rules (e.g., 24h, 7d, keep forever, delete).
Foreground Service & Notifications
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Maintains a lightweight observer to detect screenshot creation events and notifies you when actions or countdowns occur.
Biometrics
USE_BIOMETRIC		 Authenticates access to your encrypted Action Vault using your device's biometric sensor.
Wi-Fi Management (Optional)
CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE		 Used strictly when you tap the "Connect to Wi-Fi" action pill extracted from a Wi-Fi QR code or credentials screenshot.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The free version of Ephemeral uses trusted third-party developer SDKs to deliver advertisements and support app development:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive banner and interstitial ads. Google may collect and use device identifiers, general location, and app interaction data to serve ads in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more by visiting Google's Advertising & Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Purchases for Ephemeral Pro subscriptions are securely handled by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card or financial information.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Automatic Deletion: Temporary screenshots are automatically purged from your device when their expiration period (e.g., 24 hours, 7 days) elapses.
  • Manual Deletion: You can permanently delete any screenshot, clear data, or remove items from the Action Vault at any time directly within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Ephemeral immediately removes all local database records, preferences, and indexed cache from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Ephemeral is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate measures.

6. Security of Your Information

We prioritize the security of your files. Vaulted screenshots are isolated and safeguarded with hardware-backed biometric security on supported Android devices. Because your files remain on your physical device, your media is never exposed to remote server breaches or data leaks.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
